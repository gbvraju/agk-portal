# SPEC-0005: The This week section follows the weekly report's rules

- Status: Draft
- Owner: maintainers
- Requirements: REQ-105

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.
Written by the intake of one sentence, as data: "The This week section shows, for the week the facts artifact names, what was delivered, what is proven, what waits for a decision, what is at risk and the hours it cost, each line a requirement's own sentence or a fixed phrase over a facts number, and each linked to its evidence on GitHub." It is milestone M2 of ADR-0002 as amended by ADR-0006, first part (Requirements and Trend follow as their own intakes). The product owner answered three questions on 2026-10-21 and set one rule for the whole section: **the page uses the weekly report's own rules, so the page and the report never disagree.** Each part of This week is computed exactly as the agk product report computes its section of the same name, from the same facts fields. The report is a pure function of the facts document (agk SPEC-0011 rule 2) and is vendored in this repository at kit 0.32.0 as `scripts/agk/product_report.py` (byte-identical to the agk repository's file when this spec was written). The references below are to agk specs and to that script; they are read-only references (this project is not named in agk). Only the wording and the layout differ: plain business phrases, fixed and listed in rule 10, each linked to its evidence. The week is the ISO week named by the artifact, Monday to Sunday UTC; what waits for a person is as of the facts' `today`. What waits for a decision is the report's Needs you; what is at risk is the report's At risk, including its grouping by kind; the cost is the report's Cost; unproven changes stay visible. No money and no rate setting in this slice. A report rule that the facts alone cannot reproduce is an open question below; no different rule is invented.

## Rules

1. **The week.** The week is the ISO week label of the `agk-facts-<week>` artifact (SPEC-0001 rule 2), from Monday 00:00 to the next Monday 00:00 in UTC, as `week_window` of the report computes it with `--week` (agk SPEC-0011 rule 8, the opening line). The lists of what waits for a person are as of the facts' `today`. The section opens with the fixed phrase "Week {label}: Monday {start} to Sunday {end} (UTC). What waits for you is as of {today}.".
2. **Five parts, in order.** Delivered, Proven, Needs your decision, At risk, Time and review, the report's five sections in the report's order (agk SPEC-0011 rule 8; `SECTIONS` and `render_report`). A part with no line says "Nothing this week", and that is the only reason for it. A part whose source is `"unknown"` shows one line "{What} could not be read: not known yet." in place of its lines (agk SPEC-0011 rule 12).
3. **Delivered** is computed as `delivered` in the report: agk SPEC-0011 rule 9 (Delivered), as amended by agk SPEC-0017 rules 3 to 5 (a requirement counts only in the week its `done_on` falls in; a `done` requirement with `done_on` unknown is listed with the note "week it became done: not known yet") and by agk SPEC-0015 rules 1 and 3 (a week with more than ten merged pull requests shows one summary line, the week link, and no line per pull request). One line per requirement, then one line per pull request with `state` `merged` and `merged_at` inside the week, in the report's order.
4. **Proven** is computed as `proven` in the report: agk SPEC-0011 rule 9 (Proven). One line per requirement whose `status` is `done` or `in-progress`, proven or not proven by the report's reasons in the report's order, `planned` requirements counted in one closing line. The proof words are the report's `REASON_WORDS`, shown as in rule 10.
5. **Needs your decision** is the report's Needs you, computed as `inbox` in the report: agk SPEC-0011 rules 3 to 6, all six kinds in the report's order (draft specifications, proposed decisions, waiting questions, changes without a verdict at least `inbox_hours` old, overdue residuals, planned requirements with no specification), each group headed by its count.
6. **At risk** is computed as `at_risk` in the report: agk SPEC-0011 rule 10 (At risk) with its grouping by kind from agk SPEC-0016 rules 1 to 6: overdue residuals, open pull requests older than 7 days, pull requests sent back, the drift line, then the pull requests merged without a verdict or before their last commit was cleared. A kind with two or more pull requests is one line that names every one of them, in ascending order. Unproven changes stay visible: they are named under Proven and under At risk exactly where the report names them.
7. **Time and review** (the report's Cost) is computed as `cost` in the report: agk SPEC-0011 rule 10 (Cost) with agk SPEC-0018 rule 6 and SPEC-0015 rule 4. For each pull request merged in the week, the hours from `created_at` to `merged_at` rounded half-up to 0.1 and its number of `fix` verdicts (the review rounds); then the totals: pull requests merged, hours in total, review rounds in total, the number that declared `Behaviour: unchanged`, and "estimate: {N} of {M} within range" or "none declared this week" as `estimate_ending` computes it from `estimated_hours` and `actual_hours`. The sums use whole tenths of an hour, not floating point, so the page and the report round alike. Any unknown term makes the sum "not known yet" with the count of changes that lack it ("{k} of {n} changes"), never a number and never zero. Hours only: no money and no rate (Q-S0005-1 for the hours).
8. **Unknown and the dirty notice.** A value the facts mark `"unknown"` is shown as "not known yet", never as zero or empty (agk SPEC-0011 rule 12). When `dirty` is not `false` the part shows the fixed notice "The working tree was modified when these facts were made (dirty: {value})." and the facts commit as a link (agk SPEC-0009 rule 24; SPEC-0011 rule 11, the footer).
9. **Links.** Every line ends with a link to its evidence, built from the facts' `repo` and `commit` as the report builds them (agk SPEC-0011 rule 11): a pull request `https://github.com/{repo}/pull/{n}`, an issue `.../issues/{n}`, a file at the facts commit `.../blob/{commit}/{path}`, a directory `.../tree/{commit}/{path}`, and the merged-in-the-week search of a busy week. A project whose `repo` is `"unknown"`, whose facts cannot be read, or whose `schema` is not 1 shows no This week, only its card "not known yet" with the reason (SPEC-0001 rule 4; the report refuses such a document).
10. **The fixed phrases.** These, and no other sentence, are shown. `{id}` is an identifier from the facts and `{title}` is a requirement's own title; no title of a pull request, a specification or a decision, and no free text of a residual, is shown as a sentence. A count of one uses the singular.

    The phrases are the product owner's wording, with the changes ruled on 2026-10-21 (decisions log).
11. **The parity test.** The tests render This week from each fixture facts document and week, and run `python3 scripts/agk/product_report.py --facts FILE --week WEEK` on the same pair. The report's lines are parsed into items (kind, ids, numbers, dates, counts, hours, links) and compared with the page's items one by one, in order (the housekeeping line is compared by its total; the per-code counts are not shown on the page). A test changes one number in a fixture and shows that only the line that depends on it changes. A kit upgrade that changes the report's rules fails the parity test, and this spec is then amended to follow it.
12. **Fixtures and tests.** The fixtures are facts documents under `site/tests/fixtures/` that carry no token and no signed address, covering each kind of line, an empty week, a busy week (eleven merged pull requests), unknown values in each part, a dirty document and a document whose `repo` is unknown. The tests are TypeScript files named `*.test.ts` under `site/tests/`, run by the npm command in `agk.toml` `project_tests`, each carrying the marker `REQ-105 ACn` it proves. They need Python for the parity test only.

## Tests

- Rules 1 and 2: the week window and the five parts in order match the report for the fixtures; REQ-105 AC1.
- Rule 11: for each fixture and week, the items, counts, hour totals, links and their order equal the report's; REQ-105 AC2.
- Rules 9 and 10: every line is a phrase of the list, ends with a link, and no pull request title or residual text appears anywhere in the page; REQ-105 AC3.
- Rule 2: a week with no item in a part says "Nothing this week" for that part only; REQ-105 AC4.
- Rules 7 and 8: unknown values in each part show "not known yet" with the count lacking them, a sum with an unknown term is not a number, and nothing is shown as zero in its place; REQ-105 AC5.
- Rule 8: a dirty fixture shows the notice and the commit link; REQ-105 AC6.
- Rules 3 and 7: a week of eleven merged pull requests shows the one summary line and the totals with the week link, and no per-change line; REQ-105 AC7.
- Rule 9: a document with an unknown repo, an unreadable document and a schema other than 1 show no This week, only the card; REQ-105 AC8.

## Proof

REQ-105 AC1 and AC2: *the page and the report never disagree.* Rules 1 to 7 and 11. REQ-105 AC3 and AC4: *plain fixed phrases, each linked, nothing invented.* Rules 2, 9 and 10. REQ-105 AC5 and AC6: *an unknown is said, a dirty tree is said.* Rules 7 and 8. REQ-105 AC7: *a busy week stays readable.* Rules 3 and 7. REQ-105 AC8: *a project without facts shows only its card.* Rule 9. The maintainers read one live week of the page beside the same week's report issue before the milestone is called done.

## Open questions

- Q-S0005-1 The owner's words are "total actual hours". The report's Cost totals the hours from `created_at` to `merged_at` of each merged pull request (SPEC-0011 rule 10; `hours_between`), and uses the facts' `actual_hours` field (first commit to merge) only in "estimate: N of M within range". The criteria follow the report exactly, as the product owner's rule for the whole section says: the total is opening to merge, the estimate comparison uses `actual_hours`. Confirm, or say that the total should use `actual_hours`, in which case the page and the report would differ and the rule of agreement would be amended.
  Ruled 2026-10-21 by the product owner: follow the report (the hours from opening to merge), labelled honestly in every phrase, "time from opening to merge", never "cost" or "effort" alone; the Cost section is titled "Time and review".
- Q-S0005-2 The report names a requirement or a decision by its identifier (REQ-105, SPEC-0005) beside its title or alone, and the page shows the same identifiers as references, because a link needs a label and the report uses them. The criteria assume the identifiers stay visible, not hidden for plain words. Confirm, or say which to hide.
  Ruled 2026-10-21 by the product owner: the identifiers stay visible, placed after the plain sentence as a reference.

Ruled 2026-10-21 by the product owner: the phrases of rule 10 are accepted with these changes, applied above: "merged with no verdict" is "merged without a review"; "merged before its last commit was cleared" is "merged before its final review"; the drift line is "Housekeeping checks: {n} issues found." with the codes only behind its link; "declared unchanged: {u}" is "declared as not changing behaviour: {u}"; "hours in total: {h}" is "time from opening to merge: {h} hours in total"; the Cost section title is "Time and review". The rest of the table is accepted. The parity test compares items, not wording, so it is unaffected.
