# SPEC-0005: The This week section follows the weekly report's rules

- Status: Approved
- Owner: maintainers
- Requirements: REQ-105

Status is `Draft` or `Approved`. An Approved spec is owned by its reviewer group; agents propose patches.
Written by the intake of one sentence, as data: "The This week section shows, for the week the facts artifact names, what was delivered, what is proven, what waits for a decision, what is at risk and the hours it cost, each line a requirement's own sentence or a fixed phrase over a facts number, and each linked to its evidence on GitHub." It is milestone M2 of ADR-0002 as amended by ADR-0006, first part (Requirements and Trend follow as their own intakes). The product owner answered three questions on 2026-10-21 and set one rule for the whole section: **the page uses the weekly report's own rules, so the page and the report never disagree.** Each part of This week is rendered from the section of the same name in `report.json`, the data that the agk product report writes beside `facts.json` in the same artifact (agk SPEC-0022, kit 0.39.0, schema 1; schema 2 from kit 0.43.0, agk SPEC-0024; schema 3 from kit 0.50.0, agk SPEC-0026). The report's own script computed every item from the same facts and rendered its markdown from the same structure, so the page and the report agree by construction; the page computes no section and re-implements no rule of the report (amended 2026-10-22 by Amendment 6 of `docs/runs/proposed-spec-amendments-4.md`, ADR-0019; the first form of this spec had the page reproduce the report's rules, measured at about 2,300 changed lines). The references below are to agk specs; they are read-only references (this project is not named in agk). Only the wording and the layout differ: plain business phrases, fixed and listed in rule 10, each linked to its evidence. The week is the ISO week named by the artifact, Monday to Sunday UTC; what waits for a person is as of the facts' `today`. What waits for a decision is the report's Needs you; what is at risk is the report's At risk, including its grouping by kind; the cost is the report's Cost; unproven changes stay visible. No money and no rate setting in this slice. A report rule that the facts alone cannot reproduce is an open question below; no different rule is invented.

## Rules

1. **The week.** The week is the `week` of `report.json` (the ISO week label of the `agk-facts-<week>` artifact, SPEC-0001 rule 2), and the first and last day and the day the lists are as of are `opening.first`, `opening.last` and `opening.as_of` (Monday to Sunday in UTC; what waits for a person is as of `as_of`). The section opens with the fixed phrase "Week {label}: Monday {start} to Sunday {end} (UTC). What waits for you is as of {today}.". The page computes no date.

2. **Five parts, in order.** Delivered, Proven, Needs your decision, At risk, Time and review: the five keys `delivered`, `proven`, `needs_you`, `at_risk` and `cost` of `sections`, in that order. A part whose list is empty says "Nothing this week", and that is the only reason for it. An item of kind `unknown` (agk SPEC-0022 rule 5) shows one line "{What} could not be read: not known yet." in place of the lines the thing would have given, where {What} is the item's `what`.

3. **Delivered** renders the items of `sections.delivered`, in the order of the data: one line per `requirement-done` (its `title`, its `criteria` count and list, or "its acceptance criteria are not known yet", and the note "week it became done: not known yet" when `became_done` is `unknown`), one line per `pull-request-merged`, and, for a busy week, the one summary line of `pull-requests-merged` with the week link and no line per pull request (the report decides, agk SPEC-0015).

4. **Proven** renders the items of `sections.proven`, in the order of the data: `requirement-proven` ("proven", or "skipped: declared as not changing behaviour" for a `last_change.proof` of `skipped`), `requirement-not-proven` with its `reasons` joined by "; " in the order of the data, each reason a fixed phrase of rule 10 (an unlisted proof code is shown as the code and a missing one as "reason not known yet"), and the closing line of `planned-not-counted`.

5. **Needs your decision** renders the groups of `sections.needs_you` in the order of the data, each headed by its `count` ("not known yet" when the count is `unknown`) and by the fixed heading of its `group` (`spec`, `adr`, `question`, `pull-request`, `residual`, `requirement`), and its items: `draft-spec`, `proposed-adr`, `question`, `pull-request-no-verdict`, `overdue-residual`, `unspecced-requirement`. The page never shows a file path as a sentence (it is behind the link).

6. **At risk** renders the items of `sections.at_risk`, in the order of the data: `residual-overdue`, `pull-requests-open-long`, `pull-requests-sent-back` (one line that names every pull request of `entries`, in the order of the data, when there are two or more), `drift-audit`, `pull-requests-merged-no-verdict` and `pull-requests-merged-not-cleared`. Unproven changes stay visible exactly where the data names them.

7. **Time and review** (the report's Cost) renders the items of `sections.cost`, in the order of the data: one `pull-request-cost` line per merged change (its `hours` from opening to merge and its `fix_verdicts` review rounds) and the `week-totals` line (changes merged; time from opening to merge in hours in total; review rounds in total; declared as not changing behaviour; "estimate: {within} of {counted} within range" or "estimate: none declared this week"). The hours are the decimal strings of the data; the page does no arithmetic and no rounding. A value that is `unknown` is "not known yet", never a number and never zero; the page computes no count, so no "{k} of {n} changes" is shown unless the data carries the count (Q-B13-3). Hours only: no money and no rate (Q-S0005-1 for the hours).

8. **Unknown and the dirty notice.** A value that the data holds as `"unknown"` is shown as "not known yet", never as zero or empty (agk SPEC-0022 rule 6). When `notices` holds an item of kind `working-tree-modified` the section shows the fixed notice "These numbers were made from a copy with unsaved changes; treat them with care." and, from the item of kind `facts-commit`, the facts commit as a link. The other notices (`kit-version`, `metrics-issue`, `drift-audit-issue`) are not shown on the page, except that the drift line of rule 10 uses the `drift-audit` item.

9. **Links.** Every line ends with the links of its item (`links`: a pull request, an issue, a file or directory at the facts commit, or a search), shown as the report built them from the facts' `repo` and `commit`. A link is shown only if its address starts with `https://github.com/` and holds no space or quote; any other address is not shown and the line stays. A project whose `repo` is `"unknown"`, whose facts cannot be read, whose `schema` is not 1, or whose report cannot be read (rule 13) shows no This week, only its card "not known yet" with the reason (SPEC-0001 rule 4).

10. **The fixed phrases.** These, and no other sentence, are shown. `{id}` is an identifier from the facts and `{title}` is a requirement's own title; no title of a pull request, a specification or a decision, and no free text of a residual, is shown as a sentence. A count of one uses the singular.

11. **The parity test.** The tests run this repository's own `scripts/agk/product_report.py` (kit 0.39.0 or later, which has `--json`) on each fixture facts document: `python3 scripts/agk/product_report.py --facts FILE --today D --week WEEK --json OUT`, and render This week from the `report.json` it writes. They compare the page with that data item by item: every item of every section appears once, in the data's order, with its identifiers, numbers, dates, counts, hour strings and links (REQ-105 AC2 as worded; Q-B13-1 ruled). A test changes one number in a fixture and shows that only the line that depends on it changes. A kit upgrade that changes the schema number fails the document check (rule 13), and this spec is then amended to follow it.

12. **Fixtures and tests.** The fixtures are facts documents under `site/tests/fixtures/report/` copied unchanged from the kit's own fixtures (public data, no token, no signed address): one covering each kind of the closed list (`kinds`), an empty week, a quiet week, a busy week, unknown values in each section and a dirty document; the `report.json` of each is written at test time by the repository's own script (rule 11), and documents that must be refused (rule 13) are made from it in the tests. The tests are TypeScript files named `*.test.ts` under `site/tests/`, run by the npm command in `agk.toml` `project_tests`, each carrying the marker `REQ-105 ACn` it proves. They need Python 3 (as `make governance` does) and no network.
13. **The document.** The page reads `report.json` from the same archive as `facts.json` (SPEC-0001 rule 2) and checks it against the closed list of agk SPEC-0022 rules 3 to 5 before it uses anything in it: exactly the keys `schema`, `week`, `opening`, `sections`, `notices`; `schema` the number 1, 2 or 3 (schema 2, agk SPEC-0024, adds `hours_unknown`, an integer from 0 and always present, to the item of kind `week-totals`; schema 3, agk SPEC-0026, adds `counts`, an object with exactly the keys `delivered`, `proven`, `needs_you` and `at_risk`, each an integer from 0 or `"unknown"`, `count`, an integer from 0, to the four grouped kinds of At risk, and `fix_verdicts_unknown`, an integer from 0, to `week-totals`; each schema has its own closed list and no field of a later schema is allowed in an earlier one); the five section keys; every item an object of a kind in the list with exactly the fields of that kind and `links`; every date `YYYY-MM-DD`; `unknown` only where the list allows it. A document that is not JSON, whose schema is not 1, 2 or 3, or that has an extra or missing key, kind or field for its schema (schema 2 without `hours_unknown`, schema 1 with it, schema 3 without `counts`, `count` or `fix_verdicts_unknown`) makes This week absent and the card reads "Not known yet: the report could not be read."; an archive of an older kit, without `report.json`, makes the card read "Not known yet: the artifact has no report (made before kit 0.39.0)."; neither stops the facts card or the page. The text of a title is escaped before it is shown (SPEC-0004 rule 2).

## Tests

- Rules 1 and 2: the week window and the five parts in order match the report for the fixtures; REQ-105 AC1.
- Rule 11: for each fixture facts document, the items, counts, hour strings, links and their order equal the data that the repository's own script writes; REQ-105 AC2.
- Rules 9 and 10: every line is a phrase of the list, ends with a link, and no pull request title or residual text appears anywhere in the page; REQ-105 AC3.
- Rules 10 and 13: schema 3 has the seventh tile "{fix_verdicts_unknown} changes without known review rounds" after the review-rounds tile, 0 included, and the fold of a grouped At risk line whose `count` is above 5 (the data's count, never the length of `entries`); schemas 1 and 2 have neither; each schema refuses the fields of a later one; REQ-105 AC5 and AC8.
- Rules 10 and 13: schema 2 has the sixth tile "{hours_unknown} changes without known hours" after the hours tile, for every schema 2 report, 0 included, and schema 1 has five tiles; the field is an integer from 0 and always present, and a report of schema 2 without it or of schema 1 with it is refused; REQ-105 AC5 and AC8.
- Rule 13: a report that is not JSON, of another schema, or with an extra or missing key, kind or field, and an archive without `report.json`, give the card's reason and no This week; REQ-105 AC8.
- Rule 2: a week with no item in a part says "Nothing this week" for that part only; REQ-105 AC4.
- Rules 7 and 8: unknown values in each part show "not known yet" (no count unless the data carries it), a sum with an unknown term is not a number, and nothing is shown as zero in its place; REQ-105 AC5.
- Rule 8: a dirty fixture shows the notice and the commit link; REQ-105 AC6.
- Rules 3 and 7: a week of eleven merged pull requests shows the one summary line and the totals with the week link, and no per-change line; REQ-105 AC7.
- Rule 9: a document with an unknown repo, an unreadable document and a schema other than 1, 2 or 3 show no This week, only the card; REQ-105 AC8.

## Proof

REQ-105 AC1 and AC2: *the page and the report never disagree.* Rules 1 to 7 and 11. REQ-105 AC3 and AC4: *plain fixed phrases, each linked, nothing invented.* Rules 2, 9 and 10. REQ-105 AC5 and AC6: *an unknown is said, a dirty tree is said.* Rules 7 and 8. REQ-105 AC7: *a busy week stays readable.* Rules 3 and 7. REQ-105 AC8: *a project without facts shows only its card.* Rule 9. The maintainers read one live week of the page beside the same week's report issue before the milestone is called done.

## Open questions

- Q-S0005-1 The owner's words are "total actual hours". The report's Cost totals the hours from `created_at` to `merged_at` of each merged pull request (SPEC-0011 rule 10; `hours_between`), and uses the facts' `actual_hours` field (first commit to merge) only in "estimate: N of M within range". The criteria follow the report exactly, as the product owner's rule for the whole section says: the total is opening to merge, the estimate comparison uses `actual_hours`. Confirm, or say that the total should use `actual_hours`, in which case the page and the report would differ and the rule of agreement would be amended.
  Ruled 2026-10-21 by the product owner: follow the report (the hours from opening to merge), labelled honestly in every phrase, "time from opening to merge", never "cost" or "effort" alone; the Cost section is titled "Time and review".
- Q-S0005-2 The report names a requirement or a decision by its identifier (REQ-105, SPEC-0005) beside its title or alone, and the page shows the same identifiers as references, because a link needs a label and the report uses them. The criteria assume the identifiers stay visible, not hidden for plain words. Confirm, or say which to hide.
  Ruled 2026-10-21 by the product owner: the identifiers stay visible, placed after the plain sentence as a reference.

Ruled 2026-10-21 by the product owner: the phrases of rule 10 are accepted with these changes, applied above: "merged with no verdict" is "merged without a review"; "merged before its last commit was cleared" is "merged before its final review"; the drift line is "Housekeeping checks: {n} issues found." with the codes only behind its link; "declared unchanged: {u}" is "declared as not changing behaviour: {u}"; "hours in total: {h}" is "time from opening to merge: {h} hours in total"; the Cost section title is "Time and review". The rest of the table is accepted. The parity test compares items, not wording, so it is unaffected.
